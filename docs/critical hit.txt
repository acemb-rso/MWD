3) Universal Result Rules
2 — Catastrophic Cascade
Apply the location-specific cascade effect.
Then roll one additional crit on the General Crit Table.
Ignore further 2s; treat additional 2s as 12.
12 — Critical Breach
Advance the hit location’s condition by 1 step.
If already Crippled, it becomes Disabled.
If already Disabled, convert to the location’s catastrophic fallback instead.
